Privacy Policy

KettleFit app (com.kettlefit.app) · Effective 9 September 2026

KettleFit is an app for kettlebell training at home. It runs entirely on your device and collects no personal data.

What data is collected

None. The app does not collect, transmit, or store outside your device any personal data, device identifiers, location data, contacts, or usage information.

There are no accounts and no sign-up: you can use the app without providing a name, email address, or phone number.

What is stored on your device

For the app to work, it saves the following in your device storage:

  • the answers you gave on first launch — the kettlebells you own, your experience level, and your training goal;
  • your workout history: date, duration, exercises performed, sets, reps, and weight;
  • app settings — sound, vibration, metronome.

This data is never sent anywhere. It is accessible only to you and stays on the device.

Internet access

The app does not request the internet permission and is technically unable to transmit data over the network. It contains no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no third-party SDKs.

Permissions

The app requests no Android permissions — no camera, microphone, location, files, or contacts.

Sharing with third parties

There is nothing to share: the data never leaves your device, so it is not shared, sold, or disclosed to third parties.

Advertising ID

The app does not use the Android advertising ID and does not display ads.

Children

The app collects no data from any user, children included.

How to delete your data

Uninstall the app — all saved data is removed with it. To clear the data while keeping the app, go to Settings → Apps → KettleFit → Storage → Clear data.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts collecting any data, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date will be changed.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer through the app's page on Google Play — the "Contact developer" section.
